# 翻译

拆完不是终点，**重组才是得分关键**。

**简略步骤**：

1. **通读全句找连接词**（30 秒）
2. **拆分意群**（30 秒）
3. **选词义**（5-8 分钟）
4. **调整语序得出译文**（5-8 分钟）

**得分铁律**：

- **宁可意译不要字字对译 阅卷按"意思是否准确通顺"给分,不是按词对应。字字直译往往读不通,反而扣分**
- **被动句 70% 改主动 / 无主**
- **超过 20 字中文自动断句**
- **定语从句短前置、长后置**

先理解再翻译，**宁可多字少字也要通顺**

~~~
1. ​​"拆 - 译 - 调"三步法​​
​​第一步拆分​​：按连词、逗号、介词等把句子拆成小块，画竖线断句。
​​第二步直译​​：先逐词直译到草稿纸，遇到不会的词跳过也不瞎编。
​​第三步调整​​：按照中文语序重新梳理，从后往前翻的情况很常见，调整后再抄到答题纸上。
2. ​​"说人话"原则​​
不要追求优美辞藻，做到​​准确 + 通顺​​即可。
写完通读一遍，如果觉得别扭或有歧义，就适当增减词或调整语序。
初期译出"非人话"很正常，不用追求专业翻译水准，先自主输出再对比修正。
3. ​​主动句转换技巧​​
看到被动结构时，尝试加上新主语改为主动句。
例如："A product was invented" → "我们发明了一个产品"，而不是"一个产品被发明了"
4. ​​对照订正法（关键步骤）​​
写完后对照标准答案，一小句一小句比较差异
重点研究：​​词汇选择是否准确？语序是否需要调整？​​。
根据自己的错误类型有意识练习，是词汇问题还是语序问题要分清楚
十年真题用这种方法练 2-3 遍，直到译文和参考答案相差无几为止。		
~~~

**定语从句那条最值得记**,举例:

- 短(前置):`a book that I like` → "我喜欢的书"

## 范文一 · 上升/增长趋势型

> 用于:柱状图/折线图,「某事物随年份上升、增多」(覆盖真题 2022快递业务量、2023健康素养)

**The chart above clearly presents the changes in** `[the health literacy level of Chinese residents]` **from** `[2012 to 2021]`. **As we can see, the figure climbed steadily from** `[8.8%]` **to** `[25.4%]`, **registering a remarkable upward trend over the period.**

**Several factors could account for this phenomenon, among which the following are the most conspicuous. To begin with,** `[the rapid growth of the economy]` **has** `[raised people's living standards]`, **so that an increasing number of people are both willing and able to** `[pursue a healthier life]`. **Furthermore, with the wide spread of** `[the Internet]`, `[relevant knowledge]` **has become far more accessible than before — a change that reveals a profound shift in public awareness.**

**Taking these factors into consideration, it can be safely predicted that this favorable trend will continue in the foreseeable future. Only by** `[sustaining such efforts]` **can we** `[build a healthier and stronger society]`.

> 中文:图表显示[X]从[年]到[年]持续上升 → 两个原因(经济+科技/观念,三句公式)→ 预测+倒装升华。 【迁移】任何"上升题"只换 4 个槽:主题、起止数字、两个原因、结尾愿景。`reveals a profound shift` / `Only by…can we`(倒装)是闪光句。

------

## 范文二 · 对比/占比型

> 用于:柱状图/饼图,「比较各项大小、占比」(覆盖真题 2024劳动课、2025老人休闲、2026儿童户外)

**What is clearly presented in the chart above is** `[the perceived benefits of children's outdoor activities]`. **According to the data,** `[satisfying curiosity]` **and** `[improving observation]` **rank the highest, recognized by** `[54.6%]` **and** `[54.5%]` **respectively, whereas** `[strengthening parent-child bonds]` **takes up merely** `[31.2%]`.

**These figures amount to far more than cold numbers. The great weight attached to** `[curiosity and observation]` **suggests that** `[people now value cognitive growth over purely physical gains]`, **which highlights a meaningful shift in** `[their attitudes]`. **Such a change, in my view, can be largely attributed to** `[the steady rise of public awareness]`.

**In a word, the data mirror** `[a positive transformation in how people view X]`. **There is every reason to believe that** `[this rational attitude]` **will take ever deeper root in the years to come.**

> 中文:图里[最高项]和[次高项]占比最大、[最低项]最小 → 这说明[什么观念/意义](vscode-webview://0b8dv7uidifdee79s5uiu738lrsr7fkjgc2fmghgfoqb7r8i269h/suggests/highlights)→ 升华。 【迁移】任何"占比/排名题"只换:主题、最高/最低项+数字、它反映的"意义"。`suggests that…which highlights`是去中式的命门(别写 this means people think)。

## 范文三 · 问题/建议型(保险覆盖)

> 用于:负面趋势、隐患,或题目要你"给建议/对策"(防身用,如近视率↑、手机依赖、过度包装)

**As is reflected in the chart above,** `[the myopia rate among teenagers]` **has** `[risen sharply from [X%] to [Y%]]` **over the past** `[few years]` **— a trend that is, frankly, far from reassuring.**

**This worrying phenomenon can be attributed to several causes. For one thing,** `[the overuse of electronic devices]` **keeps** `[young people]` **glued to screens for hours; for another,** `[heavy academic pressure]` **leaves them little time for rest.**

**Given the analysis above, it is high time that effective measures were taken to reverse the situation. For one thing, the government is supposed to** `[guide the public toward healthier habits]`; **for another, individuals ought to** `[act responsibly themselves]`. **Only in this way can we** `[turn the tide]`.

> 中文:图表显示[坏现象]恶化 → 两个原因 → 政府+个人两层建议。 【迁移】凡是"坏事/要对策"的题用它。`it is high time that…were taken`(虚拟)+ `Only in this way can we`(倒装)双闪光句。
